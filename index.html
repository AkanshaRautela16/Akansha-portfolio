About Me

Hello! I'm Akansha Rautela, a Research Associate working with Prof. Indra Narayan Kar and Prof. Arpan Chattopadhyay in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Delhi. I completed my PhD in the Control and Automation group under the supervision of Prof. Deepak U. Patil and Prof. Indra Narayan Kar at IIT Delhi.

My doctoral research addresses optimal consensus in multi-agent systems under input, fuel, and energy constraints. The exact minimum time to consensus and the corresponding consensus point for agents with LTI system dynamics is computed. In this work, we developed a distributed framework for computing optimal consensus when each agent has a finite fuel or energy budget. Formulated the consensus problem as a set-theoretic intersection problem over the agents' attainable sets, used Helly's theorem from convex geometry to reduce the computational complexity for large numbers of agents, and applied Gröbner basis-based elimination to obtain the solution in closed form. The result is a computationally tractable method for computing optimal trajectories of continuous-time systems under strict resource constraints.

In my current project, funded by the Indo-French Centre for the Promotion of Advanced Research (IFCPAR), I develop multi-sensor set-membership filters for bearings-only target motion analysis.

Research Interests

Optimal Consensus in Multi-Agent Systems

Minimum-time and minimum-fuel consensus for agents with bounded inputs and fuel or energy budgets. Using attainable sets, Helly's theorem, and Gröbner-basis elimination, I obtain the optimal consensus time and point in closed form for double-integrator, damped second-order, and general LTI agents.

Set-Membership Estimation for Target Motion Analysis.

State estimation for bearings-only TMA using ellipsoidal set-membership filters, multi-sensor fusion, and SDP-based updates.

Constrained Multi-Vehicle Coordination

Extending the attainable-set framework to formation consensus in vehicle platoons with acceleration, velocity, energy, and inter-vehicle safety constraints.
